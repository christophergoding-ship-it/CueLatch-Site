Privacy policy

Effective 2 October 2026

This policy explains how Edge of Frame Ltd handles information when you use our production app or contact us about it.

Information used by the app

The app may access files and media that you choose to import, microphone or audio inputs when you deliberately use live-audio features, and local-network information needed to discover and communicate with another device running the app. This access is used to provide the feature you selected.

Project and production content

Projects, cue information and imported production media are intended to remain under the user’s control. Connected-device features may transmit the information required to synchronise or perform cues between devices on the network selected by the user.

Information you send to us

If you contact us for support or testing feedback, we receive the information you choose to provide. We use it to respond, investigate issues and improve the app.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Support correspondence and associated diagnostics are retained only for as long as reasonably needed for support, development, security or legal purposes.

Your choices

You control which project media you import and which device permissions you grant. Device settings can be used to review or withdraw permissions, although removing access may prevent the related feature from working.

Contact

Existing testers can use the contact address supplied with their invitation for privacy questions.
