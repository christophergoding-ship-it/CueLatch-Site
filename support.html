Support

CueLatch is currently in private testing. If you have encountered a problem, have a question about setup, or would like to share production feedback, contact us by email.

Contact

support@cuelatch.app

When reporting a problem

Including the device model, CueLatch version, what you expected to happen and the steps that led to the problem will help us investigate. Please do not send confidential production material unless we specifically request it.

Leaving prop-device mode

While the handset is running as a prop device, press the top-right corner four times to return to CueLatch.
